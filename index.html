    Source Attribution & Compliance

    axcrap is a minimalist index of open-source and publicly embeddable HTML5 games, providing direct attribution to creators and source repositories.

    Attribution Policy

    Every game card and player view links directly to the original author, source repository, or published portfolio. We do not alter game canvas code, remove credits, or monetize third-party assets.

    Third-Party Embedding

    We respect HTTP security headers including X-Frame-Options and Content Security Policy directives. Games are embedded in secure sandboxed iframes.

    Terms of Service

    1. Purpose: axcrap provides a clean, index-based interface to open web games. All games remain the property of their respective creators.

    2. No Warranty: Games are provided on an "as-is" basis as hosted by their respective third-party platforms.

    3. Acceptable Use: Users must not attempt to use this portal for unauthorized network bypass where prohibited by organizational policy.

    Privacy Notice

    Zero Tracking Guarantee: axcrap does not collect personal identifiers, sell user data, or inject ad tracking pixels.

    Local Storage: Submitted bug reports are saved solely in your browser's local storage.

    Third-Party Content: When playing an embedded game, your browser connects directly to the game's third-party host server.

    Content Removal & DMCA

    Are you a game developer or copyright owner wishing to update attribution or remove your game from our catalog?

    We honor all takedown and modification requests promptly. You can use the in-app Report Game Broken button on the game's player screen or submit an issue via our repository with proof of ownership.
